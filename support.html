Sea Battle: Local Wireless

This is a local game. Nearby matches use the local network on the two devices. There is no account and no internet gameplay.

Play vs Computer

From the main menu, choose Play vs Computer, pick a difficulty, place your fleet or tap Auto Deploy, then Ready.

Nearby multiplayer

  • Both devices need Wi-Fi enabled and should be near each other.
  • One player taps Host Game. The other taps Join Game and connects.
  • iOS may ask for Local Network access. That permission is required to find the other device.
  • Internet is not required.
