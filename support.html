Sea Battle: Local Wireless

Classic naval combat on iPhone and iPad. Play vs Computer, host a nearby match, or Play Online with Apple Game Center.

Play vs Computer

From the main menu, choose Play vs Computer, pick a difficulty, place your fleet or tap Auto Deploy, then Ready. The computer never sees your ship layout.

Nearby multiplayer

  • Both devices need Wi-Fi enabled and should be near each other.
  • One player taps Host Game. The other taps Join Game and connects.
  • iOS may ask for Local Network access. That permission is required to find the other device.
  • Internet is not required for nearby play.
  • If the connection drops for a moment, the game tries to reconnect for about 30 seconds. Stay in the app if you can.

Play Online (Game Center)

  • Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings.
  • Tap Play Online to invite a friend or use Automatch.
  • Apple processes your Game Center name and match traffic. We do not run a game server.

Settings

Music, sound effects, voice volume, speaker, and fleet artwork (Standard or Pirate) are in Settings. Gameplay rules and free-asset credits are in Rules.
