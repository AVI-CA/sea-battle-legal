Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026
App Name: Sea Battle: Local Wireless

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Sea Battle: Local Wireless (the “App”) handles your information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • No Personal Data Collection: The App has no user accounts, analytics, or telemetry. We do not collect, store, share, or process personal information on our servers, and we do not send it to third parties.
  • Local Network Communication: Nearby multiplayer uses Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity framework to connect two devices over the local network (Wi-Fi or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi). Internet is not required. Play vs Computer does not use the network at all.
  • Data Transmission: During a nearby match, the two devices exchange only session data: device display names, a temporary match identifier, shot coordinates, hit/miss/sunk results, and turn or game status. Ship layouts are never sent. No game data is sent to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party services.
  • Device names: The App uses the name of your device, as shown in iOS settings, so the other player can recognize a hosted game. That name is shown only on the two devices in the match and is not collected or stored by us.

3. Permissions

The App requests Local Network access exclusively to discover and connect with nearby devices for local multiplayer. It does not request Bluetooth, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or tracking permission. You can revoke Local Network access at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data from any users, it is suitable for users of all ages and does not collect information covered by the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
